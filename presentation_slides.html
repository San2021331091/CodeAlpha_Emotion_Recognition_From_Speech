Problem & Scope

Acoustic Emotion Recognition Challenge

Human speech conveys emotional states through nuanced vocal tract variations, prosody, and harmonic formants. Recognizing emotions computationally requires decoupling acoustic speech properties from speaker identity and linguistic words.

Target 8 Emotional Classes (RAVDESS Standard)

😊 Happy • 😡 Angry • 😢 Sad • 😐 Neutral
😌 Calm • 😨 Fearful • 🤢 Disgust • 😲 Surprised

Russell's Circumplex Affect Model

Continuous mapping of speech utterances into 2-dimensional Valence (pleasure vs displeasure) and Arousal (activation energy vs calm).

Presenter Script: "Our objective is to classify 8 emotional states from vocal signals according to the RAVDESS benchmark, mapping each prediction into Russell's 2D Valence-Arousal Circumplex Affect space."
Acoustic Signal Processing

Feature Extraction & Acoustic Engineering

Raw 22,050 Hz audio waveforms are transformed into rich acoustic feature representations via Short-Time Fourier Transform (STFT) and Mel-frequency filter banks:

1. Mel-Frequency Cepstral Coefficients (MFCCs)

40 static coefficients + 40 delta + 40 delta-delta dynamics capturing vocal tract resonance filtering and formant envelopes.

2. 12-TET Chroma Pitch Class Profile

Harmonic energy distribution across 12 pitch semi-tones to detect melodic pitch contours and emotional musicality.

3. Spectral Contrast & Tonnetz

Measures valley-to-peak decibel ratios across 7 sub-bands and tonal centroid harmonic relations.

4. Prosodic Diagnostics (F0, RMS, ZCR)

Fundamental frequency (pitch mean & variance), Root-Mean-Square intensity energy, and Zero-Crossing Rate.

Presenter Script: "We extract 231 distinct acoustic dimensions per utterance, including 40 MFCCs, delta acceleration features, 12-tone chroma profiles, spectral contrast, fundamental pitch F0, and energy dynamics."
Machine Learning Methodology

Deep Learning Architectures for Speech Emotion

We designed and evaluated multiple deep learning and statistical architectures:

1. 1D-CNN (Temporal ConvNet)

3-block 1D temporal convolution with BatchNorm, ReLU, and MaxPool1D to learn local spectro-temporal filter banks.

2. Bi-LSTM (Recurrent Network)

Bidirectional LSTM processing frame sequences forward and backward with temporal attention pooling.

3. CRNN (Hybrid Model)

Cascades 1D-CNN feature extractors into Bi-LSTM prosodic sequence layers. Selected as our Best Model!

Presenter Script: "To model both short-term acoustic timbres and long-term prosodic cadences, we implemented 1D-CNN, Bi-LSTM with attention, and a hybrid CRNN that achieves superior classification fidelity."
